# 2e Studio website pricing and care

Public build ranges and care choices share `src/packages.js` so the pricing cards stay consistent.

| Build | Typical scope | Guide price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple Site | 1–2 straightforward pages | $400–$550 |
| Business Site | 3–5 pages or a standard connection | $650–$1,000 |
| Expanded Site | Larger scope or several connections | $1,100–$1,800 |
| Custom Build | Ecommerce, memberships, CMS, migrations, custom functions | Scoped individually |

Normal sections belong to the agreed page scope. Each separate page adds its own layout and testing. Quotes depend on complexity and actual work, not page count alone.

Every build includes two consolidated revision rounds, responsive layouts, launch setup, source files and an account summary, and 30 days of defect fixes. Payments are 50% to book and 50% before launch.

| Care | Monthly | Six months prepaid | Update allowance | Response target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Website Care | $49 | $279 | 30 minutes in one batch per month | 3 business days |
| Website Support | $89 | $507 | 60 minutes across up to two batches per month | 2 business days |

Both plans manage standard static-site hosting and deployments, monthly key-link/contact-form checks, and source backup/rollback. Domains and paid subscriptions are separate. Complex applications need separate support scopes.

Unused time expires monthly. Six-month prepayment does not combine allowances. Response means acknowledging and scheduling, not a completion guarantee. Monthly cancellation takes effect before the next billing period. Unused full prepaid months refund at $46.50 or $84.50; the current month is not refunded. Prepaid care does not automatically renew.

Handoff is included with the paid build and is explained as an alternative after care. It is not marketed as a $0 monthly service. Ownership of agreed custom work transfers after full payment subject to the agreement and third-party licenses. A CMS or editing dashboard is not included by default.

The short project inquiry collects contact details and project goals, with optional website, timing, and budget details. It does not generate an automatic estimate, collect payments, or execute an agreement. The Formspree destination is preserved. Use the internal Build Estimator to prepare a reviewed quote.

The public care guide is `public/2e-studio-care-guide.pdf`. If prices or terms change, update the guide and client agreement as well as `src/packages.js` and the surrounding explanatory copy.

Build and review phone, tablet, and desktop layouts before deploying changes. Preserve existing client prices and promised care terms unless they agree to a change.
