# 2e Studio

## What it is

2e Studio creates thoughtful websites, digital tools, and creative online
experiences for small businesses, artists, performers, and people building
something meaningful.

## Primary services

1. Custom websites
2. Online sales and ordering when appropriate
3. Automation and practical digital tools
4. Customer-experience and operations support
5. Ongoing website care

## Initial customers

- Small local businesses
- Artists and performers
- Creative professionals
- Specialty retailers
- Service businesses

## Current visual direction

Warm, creative, approachable, mid-century inspired, and lightly technical.

The bird and portrait are temporary brand assets and may change.
